# Corpus Map: Viktoria Izdebska (Substack + dis-tribution.com)

Substack scraped 2026-07-31, updated 2026-08-18 (9 public posts). Her venture site dis-tribution.com extracted 2026-08-03. Full texts in `articles/`. Read the relevant article before quoting; quote her only with attribution.

**Thesis history (important for the "Where this thesis breaks" section):** her manufactured-distribution idea has moved twice. V1 (pre-Substack): build free tools, aggregate users, sell the segment. Rejected by buy-and-build buyers (untrusted quality signal; no economic validation), documented in The Only Moat Left. V2 (dis-tribution.com, mid-2026): manufactured to order; co-designed with a committed buyer (pre-sold), behaviourally qualified on usage (workflow 3x/week for 30 days converts ~3x casual signups), transferred with zero integration debt, 6 to 9 months concept to close. Unproven assumption she names herself: that users acquired this way convert at the modelled rate; "that's what the first asset tests." Analyses must engage V2, not V1.
